# Discordant 1.0.0.0

## GW2 names
- Show players' GW2 account or character names instead of their Discord names
- Optionally show a second name in brackets, e.g. `Character (Discord name)`
- Players are recognised when their Discord nickname contains their account name (`Name.1234`), or you can link them by hand in the options
- Squad data comes from RealTime API, Pie UI, or ArcDPS / Unofficial Extras (via Raidcore's ArcDPS Integration), whichever are running

Thanks to Goran Topic for contributing this feature.

## Pie UI integration
- Pie UI's party and squad frames can show who is in voice and who is speaking
- New "Overlay shows" option: everyone, only people outside your party/squad, or nobody
